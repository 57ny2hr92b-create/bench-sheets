Method

Tick each step as done

Make FR-003 while the cupcakes cool. Frost only when fully cold.

  1. 01

    Prep. Rack in the middle; heat the oven. Line the 12-cup tin with paper liners.

    —
    175 °C 350 °F
  2. 02

    Wet. In a large bowl whisk melted butter, brown sugar, vanilla and salt until smooth and glossy.

    20 s
  3. 03

    Eggs. Add one at a time, whisking until no streaks of white remain before the next.

    1 min
  4. 04

    Dry. Whisk flour, cinnamon, soda and powder; fold in with a spatula until no flour pockets remain.

    —
    Don’t overmix
  5. 05

    Carrots. Fold in the carrots and any add-ins just until the shreds are even through the batter.

    —
  6. 06

    Portion. Scoop into the liners, three-quarters full. Weigh the first cup to set the scoop.

    —
    ≈ 66 g each
  7. 07

    Bake. Until the tops dome slightly and a toothpick comes out clean with a few moist crumbs.

    17–20 min
    175 °C 350 °F
  8. 08

    Cool. Completely, in the tin. Make the frosting from card FR-003 meanwhile.

    Until cool
    45–60 min
  9. 09

    Frost. Pipe, or swoosh on with an offset spatula or the back of a large spoon.

    10 min
    ≈ 32 g each
