Method
  1. 1

    Bloom. Boil the water, add the tea; simmer until the leaves open.

    2 min
    Low simmer
  2. 2

    Infuse. Add milk and cream. Heat to steaming, then off heat, lid on.

    10 min
    85 °C
  3. 3

    Strain. Fine sieve; press the leaves hard. Weigh; top up with milk to 550 g.

    —
    550 g
  4. 4

    Cook. Whisk yolks, sugars, starch, salt; temper. Whisk over heat; boil 1 min.

    3–4 min
    Boiling
  5. 5

    Finish. Off heat, whisk in butter. Sieve into a tray, film on the surface, chill.

    Until cold
    2–4 h, 4 °C

Leaves keep ≈ 50 g of liquid; the top-up milk puts it back. Boil a full minute or the cream thins overnight. Used in PA-004.
