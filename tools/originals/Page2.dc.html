Method

Tick each step as done
  1. 01

    Brown butter. Melt in a light saucepan, stirring, until foamy, light amber and nutty.

    3–4 min
    after melting
  2. 02

    Cool. Scrape the butter and browned bits into a large bowl. Cool until warm, not hot.

    20 min
    Warm
  3. 03

    Prep. Heat the oven. Grease the pan and lay a parchment sling over the long sides (fig. 1).

    —
    165 °C 325 °F
  4. 04

    Wet. Whisk part B into the butter until smooth and glossy.

    1 min
  5. 05

    Dry. Add part C and stir just until no flour pockets remain. Stir in the dough chips.

    —
    Don’t overmix
  6. 06

    Press. Press into an even layer with oiled hands. Press the top chips in.

    —
    39 g on top
  7. 07

    Bake. Until puffed and lightly browned; a skewer shows moist crumbs or melted chocolate.

    30–45 min
    165 °C 325 °F
  8. 08

    Cool + cut. At least 1 h in the pan. Lift out by the sling and cut 6 × 4 (fig. 1).

    1 h
    24 squares
