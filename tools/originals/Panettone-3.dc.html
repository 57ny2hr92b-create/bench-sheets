Method

Day 1 evening → day 2
  1. 11

    Divide, shape. Oil the bench, halve by weight. Four slap-folds (fig. 1), round taut, into molds upright.

    15 min
    2 × 610 g
  2. 12

    Retard. Molds on a sheet inside a sealed produce bag. Refrigerate overnight.

    8–16 h
    4 °C
  3. 13

    Proof. Day 2. Out of the bag, warm and humid, until the dome peaks just over the rim. Preheat at 5 h.

    5–6 h
    30 °C
  4. 14

    Bake. Mist, pearl sugar. 20 min door shut, then drop; bake to 85 °C inside.

    35–40 min
    175 → 150 °C
    350 → 300 °F
  5. 15

    Hang. Straight out: two skewers 2.5 cm up (fig. 2); invert over the pots until fully cool.

    Until cool
    4–12 h

If it goes wrong

Most likely cause first
Butter won't takeDough too warm. Chill the bowl 15 min, add smaller pieces
No windowKnead longer; flour under 12.5% protein won't get there
Dome fellDoor opened before 20 min, or proofed past 1 cm over rim
Dense, sunk crumbCooled upright. Skewer and invert within a minute
Dry, toughOverbaked. Pull by temperature, not color
