  1. 01

    Biga. Day 1. Yeast into warm water, stir in flour, knead 1 min. Cover until doubled, then chill.

    Primo, hydrate. Yeast into warm water in the mixer bowl. Add flour and biga; stir shaggy. Cover, rest.

    10 min
    45 °C water

    Primo, knead. Add sugar, milk powder, salt, yolk. Hook on low until smooth and tight; window tears.

    12 min
    Low
  4. 04

    Primo, butter. A third on low, 2 min; rest in two adds on medium. Knead to a clear window.

    12 min
    Medium
  5. 05

    Bulk. Ball up into the 2 L tub, mark the height, lid on. Warm until tripled, then chill 1 h.

    1–2 h
    30 °C, then 4 °C
  6. 06

    Aromatics. Mix honey, zest and vanilla seeds. Soak raisins in 45 g hot water; drain before use.

    ≤ 24 h
    Room temp
  7. 07

    Secondo, mix. Whisk flour + gluten. Yeast into cold water; add primo, yolks, flour. Low until wet.

    1 min
    Low
  8. 08

    Secondo, sugar. Sugar in three adds on low, scraping down. Knead until it cleans the bowl.

    15 min
    Low
  9. 09

    Secondo, butter. 14 g at a time on low; after a third, medium. Knead to a clear window.

    10–12 min
    Medium
  10. 10

    Inclusions. Aromatics on medium until taken up. Low: peel, then drained raisins, until even.

    1–2 + 5 min
    Medium, then low
