Cream cheese frosting

Method
  1. 1

    Cream. Beat cream cheese, butter, vanilla, salt until smooth.

    1–2 min
    Medium
  2. 2

    Sugar. Add on low, a spoonful at a time; scrape the bowl.

    —
    Low
  3. 3

    Whip. High until light and fluffy; more sugar if it slumps.

    2–3 min
    High
  4. 4

    Hold. Use at once, or chill covered; beat again before use.

    —
    4 °C

One batch frosts 12 cupcakes at ≈ 32 g, or a single 20 cm layer. Frosted bakes go in the fridge. Used in CA-011.
