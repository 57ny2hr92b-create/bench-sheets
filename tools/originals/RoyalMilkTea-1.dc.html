Royal milk tea cream puffs

Craquelin choux filled with an Assam pastry cream, lightened with whipped cream. The tea opens in boiling water first, then steeps in the milk, the way royal milk tea is made.

Formula

% · each part's flour = 100

Craquelin and choux each take their own flour as 100. The filling is made from card CR-001 and sits outside.

Tea: loose Assam, broken leaf (BOP) or CTC. Grind the craquelin tea in a spice grinder.

Eggs: stop when the paste falls in a V. Dry air can take all 240 g.

† Tea strength doesn't scale in a straight line; taste before trusting a multiply.
