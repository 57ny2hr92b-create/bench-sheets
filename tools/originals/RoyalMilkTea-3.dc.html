Method

Cool and fill
  1. 10

    Cool. On a rack until cold; the shell should sound hollow.

    Until cold
    20–30 min
  2. 11

    Lighten. Whip cream to soft peaks. Beat the cold CR-001 smooth; fold in the whip in two adds.

    5 min
    Soft peaks
  3. 12

    Fill. 6 mm tip into the base hole; fill until heavy (fig. 2). Serve within 4 h.

    ≈ 33 g
    each
