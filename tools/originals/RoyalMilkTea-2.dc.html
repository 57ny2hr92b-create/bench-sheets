Schedule

One day, about 5 h
Hands-on Waiting
Start

Or make the cream and craquelin the day before; start at choux.

Method

Tick each step
  1. 01

    Cream. Make card CR-001. Film on the surface; chill until cold and set.

    Until cold
    2–4 h, 4 °C
  2. 02

    Craquelin. Beat butter, brown sugar, ground tea and salt. Add flour; mix until it clumps.

    2 min
    Low
  3. 03

    Roll, cut. Roll between parchment to 2 mm. Freeze flat until firm; cut 30 discs at 4 cm.

    30 min
    Freezer
  4. 04

    Panade. Bring water, milk, butter, sugar and salt to a boil. Off heat, add all the flour.

    —
    Rolling boil
  5. 05

    Dry. Back on medium, stir and press the paste until a film forms on the pan.

    2–3 min
    Medium
  6. 06

    Eggs. Paddle to cool. Eggs in four adds; stop when the paste falls from the paddle in a V.

    5 min
    Paste ≤ 60 °C
  7. 07

    Pipe. 4 cm mounds, 15 per tray (fig. 1), a frozen disc on each. Tray 2 to the fridge.

    10 min
    15 per tray
  8. 08

    Bake. Mid rack, one tray at a time. Door shut 20 min, then drop the heat.

    40–45 min
    190 → 165 °C
    375 → 325 °F
  9. 09

    Vent. Poke a 6 mm hole in each base. Back in the oven, heat off, door ajar.

    10 min
